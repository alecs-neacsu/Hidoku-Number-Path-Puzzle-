Privacy Policy – Hidoku: Number Path Puzzle

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Hidoku: Number Path Puzzle (package com.alexneacsu.hidoku) is developed by Alex Neacsu. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or usage data. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking services, and it does not request access to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (puzzles in progress, solved puzzles, times) and your settings are saved only on your device, inside the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. If Android backup is enabled on your device, the system may include this data in your own device backup, handled by Google under your Google account settings.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
